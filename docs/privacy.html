Privacy

Steelman is a class project. This page says what this site collects today, why, and how to turn it off.

Usage analytics

This site and its prototype send usage to PostHog, an analytics service, on its US servers: pages viewed, clicks, the names of prototype actions (such as “unfold”), and screen replays of visits. Replays hide what you type. Nothing identifies you by name or email; a random ID in your browser groups one visit's events.

We use this only to see where people get stuck, so the product can be fixed. It is not sold or shared for advertising.

Usage analytics are on in this browser.

If your browser sends Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track, nothing is sent and the notice isn't shown.

What stays in your browser

Everything you write, vote, or change in the prototype is saved in your own browser and is not sent anywhere. Clearing this site's data removes it.

Accounts

The prototype has no real accounts: signing in, email links, and deleting an account are simulated. When Steelman has accounts, this page will say what they store. The plan: your email and username; what you write, which other people on the board see; and your votes and assessments, which count toward shared totals but aren't shown as yours. Deleting an account removes your email, votes, assessments, and comments. Arguments, wordings, and sources you wrote stay on their boards, credited to deleted_account, so other people's work keeps its context.
